**How to read an entry.** Each line is `Hungarian — pronunciation — English`. The middle field
is an English-speaker's respelling, not IPA: capitals mark the stressed syllable, which in
Hungarian is *always* the first one. `ö` and `ü` are front rounded vowels; `á` is a long open
`ah`; `sz` is English *s*; plain `s` is English *sh*; `cs` is *ch*; `gy` is roughly the *d* in
*during*. Long consonants (`ssz`, `tt`) are genuinely held twice as long.

## The shape of the course

Lessons climb a seven-rung ladder from single words to extended speech. A lesson may only
climb one rung at a time and never falls back, and each rung caps how many brand-new words a
lesson may introduce — so almost everything in a later lesson is made of words taught earlier.

### Lesson 16 — The Accusative Case

*Band B1, lesson 2 of the band. Rung 5: Full sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Listen for the extra -et/-at at the end — that's the accusative doing its job.

**Grammar explained in the app at this point.**

> Hungarian marks a direct object with the accusative suffix -t (with a linking vowel — -at/-et/-ot/-öt — chosen by vowel harmony). Words without a possessive, like csapot and sampont, take it directly. When the noun already carries a possessive suffix, the two can stack: fej → fejed (your head) → fejedet (your head, as the object).
>
> With -m (my) and -d (your), that -t is optional. Mosd meg a kezed! and Mosd meg a kezedet! are both correct — the short form is what you will usually hear at home, which is why most lessons here use it. The longer form is a little more careful or emphatic.
>
> With his/her (-a/-e/-ja/-je) it is not optional: the -t is required. That is why this lesson says Fogd meg a kezét! and never Fogd meg a keze!

- **Mosd meg a fejedet!** — *mosd meg o FE-ye-det* — Wash your head!
- **Mosd meg a füledet!** — *mosd meg o FÜ-le-det* — Wash your ear!
- **Mosd meg az ujjadat!** — *mosd meg oz UY-yo-dot* — Wash your finger!
- **Nyisd ki a csapot!** — *NYISH-d ki o CHO-pot* — Turn on the tap!
- **Add ide a sampont!** — *odd I-de o SHOM-pont* — Give me the shampoo!
- **Töröld meg a füledet!** — *TÖ-röld meg o FÜ-le-det* — Dry your ear!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Fogd meg a kezét!** — *fogd meg o KE-zét* — Hold his/her hand!

New word forms here: fejedet, füledet, ujjadat, nyisd, ki, csapot, ide, sampont, fogd, kezét. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 18 — Negation in Sentences

*Band B1, lesson 4 of the band. Rung 5: Full sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Ne félj! (don't be scared) is one of the most useful calming phrases you'll ever say to a child in the bath.

**Grammar explained in the app at this point.**

> Two different negation words. Ne + imperative = a prohibition: ne állj! = don't stand!. Nem + statement = a plain negative fact: nem forró = it isn't hot. They are not interchangeable — ne always precedes a command, nem always precedes a statement.

- **Ne állj fel a kádban!** — *ne áy fel o KÁD-bon* — Don't stand up in the bath!
- **Ülj le, ne állj!** — *ewy le ne áy* — Sit down, don't stand!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Nem forró, ne félj!** — *nem FOR-ró ne féy* — It's not hot, don't be scared!
- **Ne félj, kész vagy!** — *ne féy kés vody* — Don't be scared, you're ready!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Ne öblítsd le még!** — *ne ÖB-lítsd le még* — Don't rinse it off yet!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Nem hideg a víz, ne félj!** — *nem HI-deg o víz ne féy* — The water's not cold, don't be scared!  (built entirely from words already taught)

### Lesson 19 — Past Tense

*Band B1, lesson 5 of the band. Rung 5: Full sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Listen for -am/-tam (I) versus -ad/-tad (you) on the end of the verb.

**Grammar explained in the app at this point.**

> Hungarian past tense adds -t or -tt to the verb stem, plus an ending that encodes who did it: mos → megmostad (you washed it), megmostam (I washed it). Töröl and öblít follow the same pattern: megtöröltem, leöblítettem. The igekötő prefixes (meg, le) you already know attach to the front of the whole past form. Van is irregular: its past is volt (was), as in Hol volt a kacsa?

- **Megmostad már a kezed?** — *MEG-mosh-tod már o KE-zed* — Have you washed your hand yet?
- **Én megmostam a hajad.** — *én MEG-mosh-tom o HO-yod* — I washed your hair.
- **Már megtöröltem a kezed.** — *már MEG-tö-röl-tem o KE-zed* — I already dried your hand.
- **Már leöblítettem a hajad.** — *már LE-öb-lí-tet-tem o HO-yod* — I already rinsed your hair.
- **Kész vagy, megmostad a füled?** — *kés vody MEG-mosh-tod o FÜ-led* — Are you ready, did you wash your ear?  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Hol volt a kacsa?** — *hol volt o KO-cho* — Where was the duck?

New word forms here: megmostad, én, megmostam, megtöröltem, leöblítettem, volt. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 22 — Because and So

*Band B2, lesson 2 of the band. Rung 6: Linked sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Mert answers 'why?'; ezért announces 'that's why'. Listen for which one comes first in the sentence.

**Grammar explained in the app at this point.**

> Mert (because) explains a reason, and it follows the result: mosd meg, mert piszkos = wash it, because it's dirty. Ezért (so/therefore) works the other way around — it comes before the result and follows the reason: piszkos a kezed, ezért mosd meg = your hand is dirty, so wash it. Same logic, opposite order.

- **Mosd meg a kezed, mert piszkos!** — *mosd meg o KE-zed mert PIS-kosh* — Wash your hand, because it's dirty!
- **Ülj a kádba, mert kész a víz!** — *ewy o KÁD-bo mert kés o víz* — Sit in the bath, because the water's ready!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Öblítsd le a hajad, mert szappanos.** — *ÖB-lítsd le o HO-yod mert SOP-po-nosh* — Rinse your hair off, because it's soapy.
- **Piszkos a kezed, ezért mosd meg!** — *PIS-kosh o KE-zed E-zért mosd meg* — Your hand is dirty, so wash it!
- **Hideg a víz, ezért várj még!** — *HI-deg o víz E-zért váry még* — The water's cold, so wait a bit!  (built entirely from words already taught)

### Lesson 23 — If and When

*Band B2, lesson 3 of the band. Rung 6: Linked sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Ha and amikor overlap with English 'when', but ha always carries a hint of 'if' too.

**Grammar explained in the app at this point.**

> Ha (if/when) introduces a real condition — something that genuinely might or will happen: ha kész vagy = when/if you're ready. Amikor (when) introduces a moment in time rather than a condition: amikor kész vagy = at the moment you're ready. Both use the ordinary present tense here — no special conditional form is needed.

- **Ha kész vagy, gyere ki a kádból!** — *ho kés vody DYE-re ki o KÁD-ból* — When you're ready, come out of the bath!
- **Ha piszkos a kezed, mosd meg!** — *ho PIS-kosh o KE-zed mosd meg* — If your hand is dirty, wash it!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Amikor kész vagy, mindjárt gyere ki!** — *O-mi-kor kés vody MIND-yárt DYE-re ki* — When you're ready, come out right away!
- **Ha hideg a víz, ne ülj a kádba!** — *ho HI-deg o víz ne ewy o KÁD-bo* — If the water's cold, don't sit in the bath!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Amikor megmostad a hajad, öblítsd le!** — *O-mi-kor MEG-mosh-tod o HO-yod ÖB-lítsd le* — When you've washed your hair, rinse it off!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Ha kész a víz, ülj a kádba!** — *ho kés o víz ewy o KÁD-bo* — If the water's ready, sit in the bath!  (built entirely from words already taught)

New word forms here: ha, amikor. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 24 — Complex Instructions

*Band B2, lesson 4 of the band. Rung 6: Linked sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Two instructions chained together with majd/aztán (then) — a natural way to give a whole routine in one breath.

- **Először mosd meg, aztán öblítsd le!** — *E-lő-shör mosd meg OZ-tán ÖB-lítsd le* — First wash it, then rinse it off!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Ülj le, majd töröld meg magad!** — *ewy le moyd TÖ-röld meg MO-god* — Sit down, then dry yourself!
- **Gyere ki a kádból, majd öltözz fel!** — *DYE-re ki o KÁD-ból moyd ÖL-tözz fel* — Get out of the bath, then get dressed!
- **Állj fel, majd töröld meg a hajad!** — *áy fel moyd TÖ-röld meg o HO-yod* — Stand up, then dry your hair!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Mosd meg a hajad, utoljára öblítsd le!** — *mosd meg o HO-yod U-tol-yá-ro ÖB-lítsd le* — Wash your hair, then finally rinse it off!
- **Ülj a kádba, aztán mosd meg a lábad!** — *ewy o KÁD-bo OZ-tán mosd meg o LÁ-bod* — Sit in the bath, then wash your foot!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Öblítsd le a kezed, aztán töröld meg!** — *ÖB-lítsd le o KE-zed OZ-tán TÖ-röld meg* — Rinse your hand off, then dry it!  (built entirely from words already taught)

New word forms here: majd, magad, öltözz, utoljára. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 25 — Resistance & Negotiation

*Band B2, lesson 5 of the band. Rung 6: Linked sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Tudom, hogy… (I know that…) softens a correction. De (but) holds the boundary firm right after it.

- **Tudom, hogy nem akarod, de kell.** — *TU-dom hody nem O-ko-rod de kel* — I know you don't want to, but it has to be done.
- **Tudom, hogy piszkos a kezed, de mosd meg!** — *TU-dom hody PIS-kosh o KE-zed de mosd meg* — I know your hand is dirty, but wash it!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **De kell, mert piszkos a kezed.** — *de kel mert PIS-kosh o KE-zed* — But it has to be done, because your hand is dirty.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Tudom, hogy hideg, de várj még!** — *TU-dom hody HI-deg de váry még* — I know it's cold, but wait a bit!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Nem akarod, de kész vagy már.** — *nem O-ko-rod de kés vody már* — You don't want to, but you're ready now.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Tudom, hogy nem tiszta, de mindjárt kész.** — *TU-dom hody nem TIS-to de MIND-yárt kés* — I know it's not clean, but it'll be done soon.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Tudom, hogy nem akarod a szappant, de kell!** — *TU-dom hody nem O-ko-rod o SOP-pont de kel* — I know you don't want the soap, but you need it!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Tudom, hogy nem akarod, de gyere ide!** — *TU-dom hody nem O-ko-rod de DYE-re I-de* — I know you don't want to, but come here!  (built entirely from words already taught)

New word forms here: tudom, hogy, akarod, de, kell. Everything else in this lesson is revision.


## C1 — Mastery

### Lesson 26 — Diminutives & Affection

*Band C1, lesson 1 of the band. Rung 6: Linked sentence.*

**Teaching note.** Kacsám (my duck), kis kacsám (my little duck) and diminutives like kezecske (little hand) make Hungarian sound warm, not childish — native speakers use them freely.

- **Gyere már, kacsám, kész a víz!** — *DYE-re már KO-chám kés o víz* — Come on, my duck, the water's ready!

- **Már nagyon tiszta vagy, kis kacsám!** — *már NO-dyon TIS-to vody kish KO-chám* — You're very clean now, little duck!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Gyere, kis kacsám, ülj a kádba!** — *DYE-re kish KO-chám ewy o KÁD-bo* — Come, little duck, sit in the bath!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Nézd, milyen ügyes a kis kacsám!** — *nézd MI-yen Ü-dyesh o kish KO-chám* — Look how clever my little duck is!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Kis kacsám, öblítsd le a hajad!** — *kish KO-chám ÖB-lítsd le o HO-yod* — Little duck, rinse your hair off!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Kis kacsám, töröld meg a lábad!** — *kish KO-chám TÖ-röld meg o LÁ-bod* — Little duck, dry your foot!  (built entirely from words already taught)

- **Kis kacsám, már kész a törölköződ!** — *kish KO-chám már kés o TÖ-röl-kö-ződ* — Little duck, your towel's ready!

New word forms here: kacsám, csak, milyen, kicsi, kezecske, kis, törölköződ. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 27 — Idiomatic Bath Time

*Band C1, lesson 2 of the band. Rung 7: Extended speech.*

**Teaching note.** Csupa víz vagy! (you're soaking wet!) is an everyday exclamation — leszel (you'll become/be) narrates forward into the near future.

- **Csupa víz vagy megint, kacsám, de milyen tiszta leszel!** — *CHU-po víz vody ME-gint KO-chám de MI-yen TIS-to LE-sel* — You're soaking wet again, my duck, but how clean you'll be!
- **Tudom, hogy nem akarod, de ha megmosod a hajad, kész leszel!** — *TU-dom hody nem O-ko-rod de ho MEG-mo-shod o HO-yod kés LE-sel* — I know you don't want to, but once you wash your hair, you'll be done!
- **Amikor kész leszel, nagyon tiszta lesz a kezed!** — *O-mi-kor kés LE-sel NO-dyon TIS-to les o KE-zed* — When you're done, your hand will be very clean!
- **Ha jól megmosod magad, nagyon ügyes kacsa leszel!** — *ho yól MEG-mo-shod MO-god NO-dyon Ü-dyesh KO-cho LE-sel* — If you wash yourself well, you'll be a very clever duck!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Tudom, hogy kész vagy, de még nem tiszta a hajad!** — *TU-dom hody kés vody de még nem TIS-to o HO-yod* — I know you're ready, but your hair isn't clean yet!  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Ha megmosod a hajad, majd a kezed, kész leszel!** — *ho MEG-mo-shod o HO-yod moyd o KE-zed kés LE-sel* — If you wash your hair, then your hand, you'll be done!  (built entirely from words already taught)

New word forms here: csupa, megint, leszel, megmosod, lesz. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 28 — Telling What Happened

*Band C1, lesson 3 of the band. Rung 7: Extended speech.*

**Teaching note.** Volt (was) plus a past-tense verb together narrate a finished bath — most (now/just now) anchors it to today.

- **Most megmostam a hajad, megtöröltem a kezed, és leöblítettem a lábad.** — *mosht MEG-mosh-tom o HO-yod MEG-tö-röl-tem o KE-zed és LE-öb-lí-tet-tem o LÁ-bod* — I just washed your hair, dried your hand, and rinsed your foot.
- **Először kész volt a víz, aztán megmostad a kezed.** — *E-lő-shör kés volt o víz OZ-tán MEG-mosh-tod o KE-zed* — First the water was ready, then you washed your hand.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Nagyon piszkos volt a lábad, ezért jól megmostam.** — *NO-dyon PIS-kosh volt o LÁ-bod E-zért yól MEG-mosh-tom* — Your foot was very dirty, so I washed it well.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Utoljára leöblítettem a fejed, és minden kész volt.** — *U-tol-yá-ro LE-öb-lí-tet-tem o FE-yed és MIN-den kés volt* — Finally I rinsed your head, and everything was done.
- **Tudom, hogy piszkos volt a hajad, de most tiszta.** — *TU-dom hody PIS-kosh volt o HO-yod de mosht TIS-to* — I know your hair was dirty, but it's clean now.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Ha megmosod magad minden nap, mindig tiszta leszel.** — *ho MEG-mo-shod MO-god MIN-den nop MIN-dig TIS-to LE-sel* — If you wash yourself every day, you'll always be clean.

New word forms here: most, és, minden, nap, mindig. Everything else in this lesson is revision.

### Lesson 29 — Storytelling

*Band C1, lesson 4 of the band. Rung 7: Extended speech.*

**Teaching note.** Ami (that/which) introduces a relative clause — it slots right after the noun it describes, set off by commas.

- **A kacsa, ami a vízben úszik, a tiéd, kacsám.** — *o KO-cho O-mi o VÍZ-ben Ú-sik o TI-éd KO-chám* — The duck that's swimming in the water is yours, my duck.
- **Amikor kész leszel, elmesélem, hogy milyen ügyes voltál.** — *O-mi-kor kés LE-sel EL-me-shé-lem hody MI-yen Ü-dyesh VOL-tál* — When you're done, I'll tell you how clever you were.
- **Tudom, hogy kész vagy, de ha megmosod a hajad, kész leszel.** — *TU-dom hody kés vody de ho MEG-mo-shod o HO-yod kés LE-sel* — I know you're ready, but once you wash your hair, you'll really be done.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **A kacsa, ami a vízben úszik, mindig ügyes és tiszta.** — *o KO-cho O-mi o VÍZ-ben Ú-sik MIN-dig Ü-dyesh és TIS-to* — The duck that swims in the water is always clever and clean.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **A víz, ami a kádban van, még mindig meleg.** — *o víz O-mi o KÁD-bon von még MIN-dig ME-leg* — The water that's in the bath is still warm.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Amikor megmostad a hajad, nagyon jól csináltad, kis kacsám.** — *O-mi-kor MEG-mosh-tod o HO-yod NO-dyon yól CHI-nál-tod kish KO-chám* — When you washed your hair, you did it very well, my little duck.  (built entirely from words already taught)
- **Amikor kész leszel, megtörlöm a hajad, aztán a kezed.** — *O-mi-kor kés LE-sel MEG-tör-löm o HO-yod OZ-tán o KE-zed* — When you're done, I'll dry your hair, then your hand.

New word forms here: ami, úszik, tiéd, elmesélem, voltál, megtörlöm. Everything else in this lesson is revision.
